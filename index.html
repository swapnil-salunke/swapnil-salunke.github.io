AI-enabled delivery

AI where it saves the team time

I treat AI as part of how a team ships, not a side experiment. People stay accountable for decisions. Agents take the repeatable work.

  1. Plan

    Agents help draft delivery plans, so the team starts from a clear first version.

  2. Review

    AI agents review every pull request before a person does, catching issues early.

  3. Ship

    CI/CD and test automation run the release path for the App Store and Play Store.

  4. Report

    Agents help draft stakeholder reports, so updates stay regular and cheap to produce.

I build with it myself. The Job Application Tracker is an agent skill that runs in Claude, Codex and Cursor, with GitHub Actions rebuilding its demo and attaching each release. Try the live demo.
How I build teams

Clear flows, decisions backed by usage

What I do differently as a manager, taken from running the Ericsson mobile team.

Hire the whole flow

I hired all seven engineers, including an automation engineer, so testing was part of the team from the first sprint.

Let the people closest to the work scope it

Developers and QA scope their own stories. QA turns requirements into flows with acceptance tests that the product manager and I review.

Decide with what users actually do

Pendo showed most users on iOS 16 or later, so I moved minimum support from iOS 13 to iOS 15 and the team stopped handling edge cases for under 1% of users.

Keep teams from blocking each other

Acceptance-test-driven delivery lets development, QA and automation work in parallel and ship early.

Use AI where it earns its place

AI agents review every pull request and help draft delivery plans and stakeholder reports. People take over from there.

Stay close to product and UX

I work with the product manager on the roadmap and with UX on flows, and I adopt Apple HIG and Material 3 across the apps.
